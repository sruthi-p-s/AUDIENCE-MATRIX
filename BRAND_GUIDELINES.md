- **Official Brand Name**: `AUDIENCE MATRIX`
- **Permitted Formats**: **AUDIENCE MATRIX** (All Caps, Bold or Standard Case: `Audience Matrix` in flowing sentences if necessary, but primary visual brand must be **AUDIENCE MATRIX**)
- **Positioning / Subtitle**: *Containerized OTT Audience Segmentation & Personalization*
- **Strict Prohibitions**:
  - Do NOT use *OTT Audience Intelligence*
  - Do NOT use temporary project names or placeholder branding
  - Do NOT use any alternate product names
  - Do NOT clutter headers with repeated taglines; primary visible brand is **AUDIENCE MATRIX**

## 2. Scope of Application
The name **AUDIENCE MATRIX** is strictly enforced across:
1. **Dashboard & UI**: Page title (`<title>AUDIENCE MATRIX</title>`), navigation headers, hero cards, logo mark.
2. **Documentation**: `README.md`, `REPORT.md`, API documentation Swagger/OpenAPI (`AUDIENCE MATRIX API`).
3. **Container & System**: `docker-compose.yml` service names and container descriptions, package manifests.
4. **Presentations & Reports**: Hackathon pitch decks, benchmark reports, and demo-facing text.

## 3. Visual & Aesthetic Guidelines
- **Palette**: Dark Navy / Charcoal Black background (`#090D16`), rounded surface cards (`#0F172A`), violet/purple focal accents (`#7C3AED`, `#8B5CF6`).
- **Texture**: Subtle data matrix / micro-grid backdrop, delicate 1px glassmorphic borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
- **Tone**: Serious, enterprise-grade OTT data science & subscriber intelligence platform (avoid neon gamified or sci-fi clichés).
